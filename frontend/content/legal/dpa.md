# Data Processing Addendum

**Effective {{effective}} · version {{version}}**

This addendum forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) between {{company}} ("Processor") and the customer organisation ("Controller") whenever the Controller's content includes personal data. It is intended to meet Article 28 UK GDPR.

## 1. Scope

The Processor processes personal data contained in documents, questions and notes that the Controller uploads ("Customer Personal Data") only to provide the Service.

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Subject matter and duration | Provision of the Service for as long as the Controller's account exists |
| Nature and purpose | Storage, retrieval, search, analysis by language models, report generation |
| Data subjects | Individuals named in the Controller's documents (for example employees, contacts, market participants) |
| Categories of data | Names, business contact details, job titles and other data the Controller chooses to upload. Special category data must not be uploaded |

## 2. Processor obligations

The Processor will:

1. process Customer Personal Data only on the Controller's documented instructions, which are these terms and the Controller's use of the Service, unless law requires otherwise (in which case it will tell the Controller first unless legally prohibited);
2. ensure people authorised to process the data are bound by confidentiality;
3. implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (Article 32), including encryption in transit, role-based access, private storage under random identifiers, logging of security-relevant actions and exclusion of content from logs;
4. engage subprocessors only as listed on the [subprocessor page](/legal/subprocessors), give at least 14 days' notice of changes so the Controller can object, and impose equivalent data protection terms on them;
5. assist the Controller, taking into account the nature of processing, with data subject requests, security, breach notification, impact assessments and consultations with the ICO;
6. notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data;
7. at the Controller's choice, delete or return Customer Personal Data at the end of the Service (the in-product export and deletion tools fulfil this), unless law requires retention;
8. make available information needed to demonstrate compliance and allow for reasonable audits, no more than once a year with 30 days' notice, at the Controller's cost.

## 3. International transfers

Where a subprocessor processes Customer Personal Data outside the UK, the Processor ensures a valid transfer mechanism is in place, as stated on the subprocessor page.

## 4. Controller obligations

The Controller is responsible for having a lawful basis to upload Customer Personal Data, for giving any required privacy information to data subjects, and for not uploading special category or criminal offence data.

## 5. Liability and precedence

Liability under this addendum is subject to the limits in the Terms of Service. If this addendum conflicts with the Terms on data protection, this addendum prevails.
