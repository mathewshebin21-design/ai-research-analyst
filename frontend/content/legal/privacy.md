## Automated processing

The Service uses language models to analyse research material and produce outputs about markets and businesses. It does not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects, so Article 22 UK GDPR does not apply. You can read how outputs are produced in our [AI and evidence notice](/legal/ai-notice).

## Who we share it with

We use the service providers in our [subprocessor list](/legal/subprocessors) to host the Service, authenticate users, run language models and perform web searches. They act on our instructions under written contracts. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

## International transfers

Some providers process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations (including the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified) or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Ask us for details of the safeguard for a given provider.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to receive it in a portable format. You can:

- **Download your data** at any time from Account settings ("Export my data").
- **Delete your account** and the workspaces you own from Account settings.
- **Email** {{privacy_email}} for any other request. We respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint), 0303 123 1113.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by workspace role. Uploaded files are stored under random names in private storage and are never served publicly. We log security-relevant actions and keep document text and keys out of logs.
