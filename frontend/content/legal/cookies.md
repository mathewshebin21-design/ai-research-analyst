# Cookies and Local Storage

**Effective {{effective}}**

{{product}} uses only storage that is strictly necessary to provide the Service you ask for. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, strictly necessary storage does not need consent, so we do not show a cookie banner.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sign-in session | Browser storage set by our sign-in provider | Keeps you signed in | Until you sign out or the session expires |
| `sih.workspace` | Browser storage | Remembers the workspace you last opened | Until you clear it |

We do not use advertising, analytics or social media cookies. If that changes, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.
